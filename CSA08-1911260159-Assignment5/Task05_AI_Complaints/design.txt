A5.1 Lexicon: positive/negative word sets and negators not/never/no. Add +1/-1 per word and reverse sign when immediately preceded by a negator. Extractive summary: split into sentences, remove stop words, count important terms, rank sentences and return top sentences in original order.

A trained Naive Bayes or language model may improve accuracy but costs model memory and inference time; test on locally labelled complaints first.
