A2.1 Missing PM values: fill with same-station median; if unavailable keep NULL. Median is robust to extremes. Reject negative or >999 sensor values and log them. Keep first duplicate station/date/hour and log later duplicates. Convert Fahrenheit by (F-32)*5/9.
A2.2 Min-max = (x-min)/(max-min), range 0–1; if max=min use 0. Z-score = (x-mean)/population standard deviation; if standard deviation is zero use 0. Min-max suits bounded inputs; z-score suits comparing distance from average.
A2.3 Date YYYY-MM-DD, trimmed title-case station names, REAL values stored without display rounding; reports show 2 decimals. Dropping missing values is O(n) and may bias averages; forward fill is O(n); median via sorting is O(n log n) and O(n) storage.
