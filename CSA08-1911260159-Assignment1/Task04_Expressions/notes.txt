Python evaluates multiplication before addition; parentheses make the intended order clear. ** is exponentiation and associates from right to left. Unary minus is applied to a value; for example -2**2 is -(2**2).
The binary float calculation (4.2 - 3.5) / 0.1 may be slightly above 7 due to representation. round() gives the nearest whole step. int() truncates toward zero; floor() moves down to the next integer, so for negative values int(-2.5) is -2 and floor(-2.5) is -3. For the exact examples, round gives 7 fat steps at 4.2 and -4 at 3.1; int/floor can differ if the floating value sits just below an integer.
All these arithmetic operations are O(1) time and O(1) extra space.
