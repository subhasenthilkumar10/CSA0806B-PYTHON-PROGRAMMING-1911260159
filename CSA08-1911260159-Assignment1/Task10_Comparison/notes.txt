scale_test.py measures 300, 6,000 and 150,000 payments with time.perf_counter(). Float and Decimal totals may differ by tiny fractions because float is approximate; Decimal gives exact decimal arithmetic when built from strings. The timing depends on the computer and should be copied from the student's own run.
Rounding each payment is appropriate when the bank pays each farmer to the nearest paisa per entry; the co-operative should confirm this policy. Rounding only the grand total can produce a different result.
A dictionary can cache rate_per_litre for repeated (fat, snf) pairs. Lookup is average O(1), while calculating a rate is also O(1); caching saves repeated arithmetic but uses O(k) space for k unique pairs.
